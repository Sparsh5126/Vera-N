Soil moisture sensor

Capacitive sensor, not the resistive one: it doesn't corrode in wet soil.
Reads into the ESP32's analog pin; calibrate dry air and a glass of water as 0% and 100%.
Goal: a pot that tells me when to water the tulsi plant, running for months on two AA batteries.
