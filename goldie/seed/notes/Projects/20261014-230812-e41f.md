ESP32 deep sleep notes

Wake every 30 minutes with the timer, read the moisture sensor, send the reading over Wi-Fi, then back to deep sleep.
In deep sleep it draws about 10 µA, so the batteries should last for months.
Power the sensor from a GPIO pin so it is off while the board sleeps.
