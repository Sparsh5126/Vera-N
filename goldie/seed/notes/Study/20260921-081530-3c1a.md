DBMS: normalization

- **1NF**: every column holds one value, no repeating groups.
- **2NF**: 1NF, and no partial dependency on part of a composite key.
- **3NF**: 2NF, and no transitive dependency between non-key columns.
- **BCNF**: every determinant is a candidate key.

Sir's example: Student(roll, name, course, teacher). Teacher depends on course, not on roll, so split it into two tables.
