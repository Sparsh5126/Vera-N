Kedarkantha trek plan

Dehradun to Sankri by shared taxi, about 8 hours. Then four days on the trail: Juda ka Talab, base camp, summit at sunrise, back down.
Budget: ₹9,000 each including the guide.
Book the train for 20 December. Rohan and Ishita are in; ask Kabir.
