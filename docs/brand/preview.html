Vera N mark

Two strokes, two notes, meeting in one drop of ink. Drawn from the rules below by tool/brand/make_brand_marks.dart, so it can be redrawn exactly. It is the app icon on Android and Windows.

Construction

  1. The outer contour is an inverted equilateral triangle, so both strokes run at 60° to the baseline.
  2. Terminals are cut flat, on the triangle's top edge.
  3. The left stroke is the heavy one, 4 : 3 against the right, like a pen's down-stroke and up-stroke.
  4. Each stroke keeps its weight down most of its length, then narrows to a neck where the two meet.
  5. The drop's diameter is the light stroke's width. It hangs just below the apex.

Widths are measured across the stroke, in units of a mark 80 tall from terminals to apex.

Two cuts

Primary. For about 96 px and up: the wordmark, print, the splash screen.

Heavy 12 · light 9 · neck 1.4 · fullness 0.3 · drop ⌀ 9

Icon cut. For the launcher icon and anything smaller: heavier, with a neck wide enough for 24 px.

Heavy 16 · light 12 · neck 4.5 · fullness 0.2 · drop ⌀ 12

Watch the neck above the drop as the size falls.

Adaptive icon

Icon cut, 48 dp wide, on stock #F5F4F6. It reaches 30.67 dp from the centre (the limit is 33). The point 42% down the mark sits on the centre, since a V carries its weight high.

On a home screen

Sizes are CSS pixels. A laptop at 100% shows about a third of the pixels a phone gives a 48 dp icon, so this is a harsher test than the phone.

Themed icon (Android 13 and later)

The system tints the monochrome layer (icon-monochrome.svg) with the wallpaper's colours. These two tints are examples.

What to look for

  • At 24, does the primary's neck vanish while the icon cut's holds?
  • Does the V look centred in the circle, or does it sit low?
  • On the photo wallpaper, does the pale plate keep it clear?
  • Then install it on the phone: the real test is a 48 dp icon among your other apps.
